<section id="about" class="about-section" markdown="1">

Hi, I'm a first-year CS PhD student at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), advised by Professor [Wojciech Matusik](https://people.csail.mit.edu/wojciech/) in the [Computational Design & Fabrication Group](https://cdfg.mit.edu/), which builds AI for the physical world. My research focuses on **AI for scientific discovery**.

Before my PhD, I worked in machine learning for two years as a Research Scientist and the sixth employee at [Cellular Intelligence](https://www.cellularintelligence.com/) (raised $60M+), training multimodal foundation models for the life sciences. Before that, I earned a BEng in Mechanical Engineering and an MSc in Computer Science with Distinction from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/). Along the way, I did research at Harvard with [Prof. Stephanie Gil](https://seas.harvard.edu/person/stephanie-gil) on transformer attention for spatio-temporal forecasting, and twice at Caltech with [Prof. Aaron Ames](http://ames.caltech.edu/) on generative models for safe robotic control. I was also selected for the [Fulbright Graduate Student Award](https://fulbright.edu.pl/laureates-of-the-fulbright-graduate-student-award-2025-26/) from Poland.
