<section id="about" class="about-section" markdown="1">

Hi, I'm a first-year CS PhD student at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), advised by Prof. [Wojciech Matusik](https://cdfg.mit.edu/). My research focuses on **AI for scientific discovery**.

Before my PhD, I was a Research Scientist and one of the first employees at [Cellular Intelligence](https://www.cellularintelligence.com/). I trained multimodal foundation models for the life sciences \[[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02726 "A measurement noise scaling law for cellular representation learning"), [2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=NO8UEX4dMb "Scaling up Measurement Noise Scaling Laws"), [3](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.05.690830v1 "Large-Scale Benchmarking of Gene and Expression Encoding Strategies for Single-Cell Foundation Models")\].

I earned an MSc in Computer Science with Distinction from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/). My thesis advisor was [Prof. Yingzhen Li](https://yingzhenli.net/home/en/). During my master's, I did a research placement at Harvard with [Prof. Stephanie Gil](https://seas.harvard.edu/person/stephanie-gil), working on transformers for time series. Earlier, I earned a BEng in Mechanical Engineering, also from Imperial. I received the SURF research fellowship and interned twice at Caltech under [Prof. Aaron Ames](http://ames.caltech.edu/), working on generative models for safe robotic control. I was also selected for a [Fulbright scholarship](https://fulbright.edu.pl/laureates-of-the-fulbright-graduate-student-award-2025-26/) from Poland.
