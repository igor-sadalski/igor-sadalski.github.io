<section id="about" class="about-section" markdown="1">

Hi, I'm a first-year CS PhD student at [MIT CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/), advised by Prof. [Wojciech Matusik](https://cdfg.mit.edu/). My research focuses on **self-improving systems** that automate algorithmic \[[1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14844 "AutoAssumption")\] and scientific discovery.

Before my PhD, I was a Research Scientist and one of the first employees at [Cellular Intelligence](https://www.cellularintelligence.com/). There, I worked on foundation models for virtual cell modeling \[[2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01332-7 "Measurement Noise Scaling Law"), [3](https://openreview.net/forum?id=NO8UEX4dMb "Scaling Up Noise Scaling Laws"), [4](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2025.12.05.690830v1 "Single-Cell Encoding Benchmark")\].

I earned an MSc in Computer Science with Distinction from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/). My thesis advisor was [Prof. Yingzhen Li](https://yingzhenli.net/home/en/). During my master's, I did a research placement at [Harvard](https://seas.harvard.edu/) with [Prof. Stephanie Gil](https://seas.harvard.edu/person/stephanie-gil), working on generative spatio-temporal forecasting for multi-agent systems. Earlier, I earned a BEng in Mechanical Engineering, also from Imperial. During undergrad, I twice received the SURF research fellowship and interned at [Caltech](https://www.cms.caltech.edu/). I worked under [Prof. Aaron Ames](http://ames.caltech.edu/) on generative models for safe robotic control \[[5](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10611355 "Generative Modeling of Residuals")\]. I was also selected for a [Fulbright scholarship](https://fulbright.edu.pl/laureates-of-the-fulbright-graduate-student-award-2025-26/) from Poland.
